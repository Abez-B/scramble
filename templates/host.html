How hosting works

This console stays on your laptop

Controls, settings and the answer list live here. Nothing on this screen is meant for the audience.

The projector view opens in its own window

Drag it to the projector. It shows the QR code, the room code and the round status — and it has no answers and no buttons.

Players join on their phones

They scan, pick a name, and get a team color and letters when you deal. Latecomers join from the next round.
